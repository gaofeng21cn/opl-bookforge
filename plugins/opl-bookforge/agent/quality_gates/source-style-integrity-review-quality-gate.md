# Source And Style Integrity Review Quality Gate

Pass conditions:

- Material claims, figures, tables, captions, callouts, and case boxes have source locators, evidence classes, and claim-ledger coverage.
- Claim language matches evidence class: constructed scenes, typical scenarios, documented process material, authorized material/interviews, owner-supplied sources, and outcome/impact evidence are not upgraded for narrative smoothness.
- Style calibration records adopted rules, rejected patterns, fatigue scan evidence, accepted exceptions, and back-propagation refs when owner-inspectable style inputs exist.
- Visible manuscript refs pass internal-language, AI-flavor, and sentence-integrity scans, or accepted exceptions are recorded with locations and reasons.
- Narrative devices follow the owner-approved style contract and supplied samples; textbook or educational framing alone does not require teaching scenarios, case boxes, or classroom language.
- Reader-facing evidence boundaries are stated through the actual population, design, endpoint, validation stage, and intended use; preventive disclaimers and generalized caveats are removed, while precise distinctions and safety facts are retained.
- Quantitative claims preserve analysis units, denominators, study design, timepoint, reference standard, endpoint, and validation cohort; cross-study comparisons state the relevant differences that affect interpretation.
- Source-dependent claims remain traceable through the source map and house-style citations; numeric citation order and cited-reference coverage are reconciled when applicable.
- Paragraph endings state findings, implications, or next conditions directly, with essential negation retained and repetitive defensive endings revised.
- Serious owner/reviewer/meta-review findings are routed through a revision entrypoint decision before prose edits are claimed.
- Meta-review loops are bounded. Budget exhaustion with a readable manuscript returns `completed_with_quality_debt` and advances; zero, corrupt, or unreadable review output becomes a no-output/failure diagnostic and also advances. Typed blockers remain reserved for unavailable executors or hard authority/safety/permission/identity/currentness/irreversible-action/human-decision boundaries.

Quality-debt conditions:

- Unsupported outcome, impact, success, validation, learning-effect, adoption, scale, or performance claims appear without matching evidence.
- Reviewer/source/style findings are answered only by a prose-polish note while durable refs remain stale.
- The stage claims review-PDF readiness, publication-proof readiness, final-export readiness, book quality acceptance, or owner acceptance.

These findings block quality/publication/export claims, not stage transition, when a readable manuscript artifact exists.
