# Chapter Materialization Quality Gate

Pass conditions:

- Every drafted chapter consumes an active task card, chapter context pack, target budget, reader-style contract, author/source stance boundary, and source refs.
- Chapter extent uses the unit and inclusion scope recorded in the approved target; planning, drafting, and QC use the same measure, separately from rendered page counts.
- First visible drafts read as book prose for the declared readers. Planning scaffolds, target budgets, QC notes, blocker refs, manifest paths, and internal workflow language stay outside manuscript body.
- Chapter openings and major transitions follow the reader-style contract and supplied samples; scenes, reader problems, consequences, and practical questions are options, not a universal sequence.
- Teaching scenarios, case boxes, classroom framing, and worked examples appear only when the approved style contract or explicit owner decision calls for them.
- Reader-facing evidence boundaries identify the actual study population, design, reference standard, endpoint, validation stage, or intended use instead of relying on preventive disclaimers and generalized caveats.
- Evidence-dense first drafts connect findings to the chapter's reader question or decision path; quantitative results preserve their analysis unit, denominators, cohort split, input timepoint, reference standard, endpoint, and validation setting.
- When several studies are compared, their relevant populations, inputs, timepoints, endpoints, and designs are made explicit enough for the comparison; the manuscript does not collapse different analysis units or denominators.
- Source-supported claims retain traceable source linkage; where numeric citations are used, in-text order and reference-list use are reconciled before handoff.
- Style QC reviews paragraph endings for repeated defensive negation; precise category distinctions, methodological conditions, and safety facts remain.
- Below-target prose is labeled `seed_in_progress` or `draft_in_progress`, not `chapter_draft_ready`, done, complete, or final.
- Chapter repair updates reusable durable refs when a finding is systemic, and keeps local prose edits local when the repair is bounded.
- Markdown chapter refs remain the manuscript source of truth; assemblies are generated refs, not the only creative working file.

Quality-debt and claim-closed conditions (not progression gates):

- Manuscript body is embedded primarily in Python, TypeScript, shell, or JSON literals.
- A chapter is marked ready while target extent, task-card linkage, reader-entry plan, source boundary, or required figure/table obligations are missing.
- Drafting claims source/style integrity, independent meta-review pass, publication-proof readiness, final-export readiness, book quality acceptance, or owner acceptance.

Partial, thin, structurally imperfect, zero, or corrupt output becomes a consumable artifact or no-output/failure diagnostic that Codex may carry forward or route back. Only an unavailable executor or a real authority, safety/permission, identity/currentness, irreversible-action, or human gate may hard-stop progression.
