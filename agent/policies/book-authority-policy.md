# Book Authority Policy

Book truth, style decisions, manuscript quality, publication proof, and export acceptance remain owned by OPL Book Forge. OPL transports refs and receipts; it does not infer publication quality from runtime completion.

Keep reusable writing methods in the product knowledge pack, user preferences
with their user's chosen source, cross-book methods with their topic, and
book-specific decisions with the project owner. Existing personal libraries and
ordinary working directories are equal source locations. Content kind and
freshness are independent of ownership scope; a dated observation does not
become a durable writing rule by being copied into memory.

Each object has one editable source. Framework provides source binding, scoped
selection, frozen context, and accepted-writeback mechanics. Book Forge owns
semantic curation and the domain acceptance decision. Manuscripts, source
evidence, and non-reproducible book deliverables are business assets; task-cache
cleanup must not delete them or their bound personal sources.
